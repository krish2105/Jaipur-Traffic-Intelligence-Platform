Read this aloud · 10 minutes

Speaking script

Each block has the English line first, then the same thing in Hinglish. Use whichever the room is speaking. Do not read both.

Opening (60 seconds)

Sir, I want to start with one number from your own department. In 2025 Jaipur had fewer road accidents than in 2024. Accidents came down 5.6 percent. But deaths went up 3.1 percent.

So the roads got safer to drive on, and more dangerous to crash on. That is 34.7 deaths for every hundred accidents. It is the worst in five years.

Sir, main aapke hi department ke ek number se shuru karta hoon. 2025 mein Jaipur mein accident kam hue, 5.6 percent kam. Lekin maut 3.1 percent badh gayi. Matlab sadak par takkar kam ho rahi hai, par jo takkar ho rahi hai usme jaan zyada ja rahi hai. Paanch saal ka sabse kharab aankda hai.

Pause here. Let the number sit. Do not rush into the solution.

The problem (90 seconds)

Now the second number. 87.86 percent of our challans are for over-speeding. Only 6.67 percent are for riding without a helmet.

Speed challans reduce how many accidents happen. That number is already improving. Helmet challans reduce how many accidents kill. That number is getting worse. We are putting 88 percent of our effort into the problem that is already being solved.

Ab doosra number. Humare 88 percent challan speed ke hain. Sirf 6.7 percent helmet ke. Speed ka challan accident kam karta hai, aur accident toh pehle se kam ho rahe hain. Helmet ka challan jaan bachata hai, aur maut badh rahi hai. Matlab humari 88 percent mehnat us problem par lag rahi hai jo already theek ho rahi hai.

Why nobody saw it (60 seconds)

Sir, this is not anybody's mistake. It is invisible with the tools we have. Google Maps measures delay. Cameras count vehicles. Signals count vehicles.

Not one of them records that 61 out of every 100 vehicles in Jaipur is a two-wheeler. And nationally, 73 percent of two-wheeler deaths are riders without a helmet. If you only measure speed, you cannot see a helmet problem.

Sir, ye kisi ki galti nahi hai. Jo tools hain unse ye dikhta hi nahi. Google Maps delay naapta hai. Camera gaadi ginta hai. Signal gaadi ginta hai. Koi ye nahi dekhta ki Jaipur mein 100 mein se 61 gaadi dopahiya hai, aur poore desh mein dopahiya ki 73 percent maut bina helmet wale riders ki hoti hai.

What we built (90 seconds)

We measure what the traffic is made of. Twelve vehicle types, converted to PCU using IRC:106, the same standard your engineers already use.

From that, one recommendation: move helmet enforcement from 6.7 percent to 23 percent, and speed from 88 down to 74. Our model says that saves about 18 lives a year. And it costs nothing. Same officers, same number of challans. Only the mix changes.

Hum ye naapte hain ki traffic mein hai kya. Baarah tarah ki gaadiyan, PCU mein badalke, IRC:106 ke hisaab se, wahi standard jo aapke engineer use karte hain. Isse ek sifarish nikalti hai: helmet ka challan 6.7 se 23 percent karo, speed ka 88 se 74. Isse lagbhag 18 jaan har saal bachegi. Aur kharcha zero hai. Wahi staff, utne hi challan, sirf mix badalna hai.

The live answer (90 seconds)

Have the console open on this. Point at the top panel while you talk.

Sir, the question every control room asks is not "how is this road". It is "how bad is Vaishali Nagar right now, and how much traffic is sitting in it". Nothing in Jaipur answers that today.

This does. We take the live speed on each road, and traffic engineering has connected speed to the number of vehicles since the 1930s. So we can tell you how many vehicles are inside a police station area right now, against how many that area can hold before it stops moving.

And it costs nothing. Twenty-five calls, every forty-five minutes, on a free account. Fifteen thousand a month against an allowance of twenty thousand.

Sir, control room ka sawaal ye nahi hota ki "ye sadak kaisi hai". Sawaal hota hai "abhi Vaishali Nagar mein kitni gaadi khadi hai". Aaj Jaipur mein iska jawab kahin nahi milta. Yahan milta hai. Har sadak ki live speed lete hain, aur speed se gaadi ki ginti nikalna traffic engineering mein 1930 se hota aa raha hai. Toh hum bata sakte hain ki kisi thana ilaake mein abhi kitni gaadi hai, aur wo ilaaka kitni jhel sakta hai. Aur iska kharcha zero hai. Pachees call, har paintalees minute, free account par.

The honest part (60 seconds)

This is the most important block. Do not skip it. It is what separates you from every vendor who has pitched them before.

Sir, I want to tell you two things against my own interest.

First. That number I just showed you, we tested it against a simulation where the true answer is known. It failed. The constant every textbook gives made us 63 percent wrong on a busy road. We refitted it against fifty-four runs and now it is 2.8 percent. On a quiet road it is still 65 percent wrong, so on a quiet road we print no number at all. The screen goes blank on purpose. Nobody would have found that from looking at the screen. We found it because we checked.

Second. We ran a standard detection model on real photographs of Jaipur streets. For a while we believed it found zero two-wheelers, and I would have said that to you today. It was our own bug — one wrong word in a lookup table — and we found it by checking a result that was too bad to be true. The honest number is 53 percent two-wheelers, against a fleet that is 61 percent. What the model genuinely cannot do is name an auto-rickshaw. The class does not exist in it. Autos are 6.2 percent of the traffic we measured. Any vendor showing you a demo has that same gap. Most will not tell you.

Sir, main apne khilaaf do baat bataunga. Pehli: abhi jo ginti dikhayi, usko humne simulation par test kiya jahan sahi jawab pata tha. Wo fail ho gayi. Kitaab ka standard number humein vyast sadak par 63 percent galat kar raha tha. Chauvan run par dobara fit kiya, ab 2.8 percent hai. Halki sadak par abhi bhi 65 percent galat hai, isliye halki sadak par hum koi number dikhate hi nahi. Screen jaan-boojhkar khaali rehti hai.

Doosri: humne standard model Jaipur ki asli photos par chalaya. Kuch samay tak humein laga ki dopahiya ek bhi nahi mila, aur main aaj aapko yahi batata. Wo hamari apni galti thi — ek table mein ek shabd galat tha. Humne pakda kyunki result itna kharab tha ki sach lag hi nahi raha tha. Sahi number hai 53 percent dopahiya, aur bede mein 61 percent hai. Model jo sach mein nahi kar sakta wo hai auto-rickshaw pehchanna — us model mein ye category hai hi nahi. Auto humare maape gaye traffic ka 6.2 percent hai. Jo bhi vendor demo dikhayega, uske paas yahi kami hai. Zyadatar batayenge nahi.

The ask (45 seconds)

One corridor. Ninety days. Paid. And the ask got smaller while we were preparing this.

Jaipur already publishes the locations of its vehicle classification cameras to the national data exchange, under an API approved by the Bureau of Indian Standards. The cameras that count and classify vehicles are already installed and already listed. What we cannot do is read what they see. That is a token, not a purchase.

So: a read token, and FIR-level accident records. About 3,000 rows a year, and it turns our estimate into a proper Jaipur model. Neither costs you money.

Ek corridor. 90 din. Paid. Aur maang pehle se chhoti ho gayi hai. Jaipur apne vehicle classification camera ki location pehle se national data exchange par publish karta hai, us API par jise Bureau of Indian Standards ne approve kiya hai. Camera lage hue hain aur list mein hain. Hum sirf ye nahi kar paa rahe ki wo kya dekh rahe hain. Ye token ka maamla hai, kharid ka nahi. Toh do cheez: read token, aur FIR-level accident record. Dono mein aapka paisa nahi lagta.

What to show, in this order

Ten minutes. Do not wander. If a page is slow, keep talking and come back to it.

1. /en/brief Open here, not on the console. It is the argument on one page and every number has the address of the endpoint that produced it printed underneath. Say "click any of these and you get the raw data" and then actually click one.

3. Live probe speeds 90 of 90 links, 25 calls, and what is left of the free allowance. This is the panel that proves it is running, not staged.
4. Jaipur's own data Scroll to it. VCC and ANPR are in pink. This is the ask, on screen, read live from the national exchange.
5. Scheme appraisal ₹184.87 crore. Through traffic and local traffic reported separately. Use this if a JDA person is in the room.
6. Live data readiness 4 of 8 live. Close on this. It is the most honest screen in the product and it names exactly what you are asking for.

Two things will look wrong and are not. Every area says "lightly loaded, count withheld" during the day, because the estimator is only trusted above 40 percent saturation and a Jaipur afternoon is below it. Say so before they ask. And Travel time reliability says "collecting, needs 39 more sweeps" because it has been recording since yesterday. That is the gate working.

If they ask the hard questions

"Ye ginti asli hai ya andaaza?"
Andaaza hai sir, aur screen par "estimated" likha hai. Speed asli hai, ginti uss speed se nikali hai. Vyast sadak par 2.8 percent farak, aur wo humne simulation par naapa hai jahan sahi jawab pata tha. Halki sadak par bharosa nahi, isliye wahan hum number dikhate hi nahi. Camera ka access mil jaaye toh yahi andaaza asli ginti ban jaata hai.

"Sirf 6 ilaake dikha rahe ho, baaki 16 ka kya?"
Sahi pakda sir. Solah ilaakon mein sadak hai par naap nahi hai, aur screen par likha hai "unmeasured, which is not clear". Matlab khaali nahi, bin-naapa hua. Wo hamari taraf ka kaam hai, aapki taraf ka nahi.

"Aap TomTom ka data hi bech rahe ho?"
Nahi sir. TomTom sirf speed batata hai, ginti nahi, aur ye nahi batata ki bus hai ya scooter. Wahi kami hai jise ye product bharta hai. TomTom humara input hai, product nahi.

"Kal se chala sakte hain?"
Jo abhi dikh raha hai wo aaj chal raha hai, aur uska kharcha shunya hai. Naya kuch nahi lagana. Camera ka token milte hi andaaza asli ginti ban jaayega.

"ITMS already lagaya hai, aapki kya zaroorat?"
Sir, ITMS signal chalata hai, aur achha chalata hai. Wo ye nahi batata ki jaan kyun ja rahi hai. Hum uske upar baithte hain, uske khilaaf nahi. Uska data humara input hai.

"Aapka data asli hai ya banaya hua?"
Accident aur challan ke saare number sarkari published hain, aur har number ke saath uska source link product mein dikhta hai. Per-road hourly count abhi simulated hai aur screen par "Simulated" likha aata hai. Hum kabhi mix nahi karte.

"Signal apne aap chalega kya?"
Nahi sir, aur ye jaan-boojhkar hai. System sujhav deta hai, officer approve karta hai, aur uska naam record hota hai. Kisi bhi badi durghatna ke baad ye sawaal aata hai ki timing kisne set ki thi. Uska jawab hona chahiye.

"Kitna time lagega?"
Corridor par 90 din. Enforcement wali sifarish aap agle hafte se laagu kar sakte hain, uske liye humara system chalu hone ka intezaar nahi karna.

Close (20 seconds)

Sir, Jaipur already collects ₹32 crore in fines and recovers 76 percent of it, the best in India. The enforcement machine works. We are only asking you to point it at the violation that is actually killing people.

Sir, Jaipur pehle se ₹32 crore challan vasoolta hai, aur 76 percent recovery hai, poore desh mein sabse achhi. Machinery kaam kar rahi hai. Hum sirf ye keh rahe hain ki use us galti par lagaiye jo asal mein jaan le rahi hai.
