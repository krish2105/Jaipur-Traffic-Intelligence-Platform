Jaipur · Traffic decision intelligence

What we are asking for

One corridor. Ninety days. Read access to data the department already owns.

The whole ask in one sentence. Give us read-only access to feeds you already operate, on one corridor, for ninety days — and at the end you get a measured result, every number carrying its source, and a written report that includes whatever did not work.

The six asks, easiest first

What we needWho signsWhat it unlocks
1 An IUDX consumer token for the Jaipur instance Smart City / IUDX nodal officer Jaipur already publishes its Vehicle Classification Camera and ANPR locations to the national exchange, under an API approved by the Bureau of Indian Standards. The locations are public; only read access is missing.
2 Read-only RTSP on six cameras, ninety days Abhay / ICCC Vehicle counts and class mix — the number nobody in Jaipur has today.
3 FIR-level accident records, about 3,000 rows a year Traffic police Severity modelling specific to Jaipur instead of state-wide averages.
4 The challan / violation feed Traffic police Enforcement reallocation computed on live data rather than published mix.
5 A named nodal officer Department Decisions instead of a queue — the ask that most often decides whether a pilot finishes.
6 One named corridor JDA / Police Somewhere to measure. We propose one already modelled.

What we are not asking for. Cameras. Hardware. Infrastructure budget. Control of any signal. The hardware already exists — 253 of Jaipur's 423 junctions are being wired for ITMS right now, and those junctions are the boundary we would measure across. We want a data feed, not a purchase order. Signal output stays advisory and an officer approves it; that is a rule in the system, not a promise.

What you get at ninety days

  • Measured vehicle counts and class mix on one corridor, with published accuracy — not a demo reel.
  • A before-measurement on the Gopalpura Bypass elevated road, locked in before the work order, so the scheme can be judged afterwards.
  • An enforcement reallocation with a lives-saved estimate, runnable by existing staff at no extra cost.
  • Every figure carrying its source, in Hindi and English, working offline.
